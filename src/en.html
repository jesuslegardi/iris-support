If a guest account has no recoverable identifier or linked sign-in, we may be unable to identify it safely; we will explain what can be verified. Full privacy and retention details

1Accounts and signing in

Why is there no password?

In versions that offer guest entry, you can start Iris as a guest without supplying an email address. Firebase creates an anonymous account identifier so your setup, habits, goals and optional coaching belong to you. Guest does not mean that data stays offline. You can continue with the free tracker. Saving your account with Apple or Google is optional.

I signed in with Google and my habits are gone

Saving a guest account with an unused Apple or Google sign-in links that sign-in to the same Iris identifier and keeps your guest data. If that sign-in already belongs to an Iris account, Iris opens that existing account; it does not merge the two accounts or their private data. Use your original sign-in method to recover an existing account. Without a saved sign-in, access to guest data can be lost if the app or its credentials are removed.

Apple gave Iris a strange email address

That is “Hide My Email”. Apple gives Iris a relay address instead of your own, and mail sent to it reaches you. Nothing in Iris is affected. If you would rather we saw your real address, you can change it for Iris in your iPhone’s Settings under your name, then Sign in with Apple.

How do I change my username, name, biography or picture?

Menu, then your profile. The username must be unique and at least three characters. Changing your display name updates it everywhere it appears, including in your friends’ lists and any competition you are in.

How do I delete my account?

2Habits and points

What is the difference between the two lists?

One list is for habits you are building — things you want to do. The other is for habits you are breaking — things you want to stop. They are scored the same way but they read differently, and Iris treats them differently when she is helping you design one.

How are points worked out?

Each week is worth the same total, and that total is divided across everything you scheduled for the week according to the priority you gave each habit. A habit scheduled four times is worth a quarter of its weekly value on each of those days. You never have to do the arithmetic — the number on the habit is what that day is worth.

Do I lose points for missing a habit?

No. Never. A missed habit earns nothing; it does not take anything away. Points only ever go up. This is deliberate: a system that punishes you for a bad week makes the next week harder, which is the opposite of the point.

What is the fuel gauge?

A short-term read on how the current stretch is going, from empty to full. It responds to the last few days rather than to your whole history, so it recovers quickly when you do.

When does the week change over?

At midnight in your own time zone, at the end of Sunday. The week that has just finished is archived whole — its habits, its marks, its notes and its photographs — and that archive is what your history, streaks and consistency figures are built from.

Can I schedule a habit at a particular time?

Yes. A habit can have no time, a single time, or a window between two times. The card shows the time and the calendar button opens the week so you can see the shape of a day. When you set a time, that also sets the day — the two cannot disagree.

What is the description on a habit for?

Anything you want to remember about it: why you started, what counts as done, what to do when it is hard. It is private to you. Iris can read one of them, and only when a message you send her names that habit and no other.

3Photographs

Why can I not choose a photograph from my gallery?

Because a photograph is evidence, and evidence you could have taken at any time is not evidence. The camera opens inside Iris and that is the only way in. This will not change.

Where are my habit photographs kept?

On your phone, and nowhere else. They are not uploaded and we never see them. What we store is a short note that a photograph exists for that day, so that your second device knows to show a placeholder rather than nothing.

Habit tracking and unlimited full-quality local habit photographs are free. Taking a photograph does not subscribe you to coaching. Only a copy you explicitly share in a competition is uploaded for the other participants.

Can I keep a copy in my normal photo gallery?

Yes. After every photograph Iris asks whether to keep it in Iris only, or in Iris and your gallery. Choose the gallery and a copy goes into your camera roll, where your usual photo backup will pick it up like any other picture.

Will my photographs survive a new phone?

On iPhone, yes — they are part of your iCloud device backup, so restoring the backup restores them.

On Android, they are deliberately kept out of cloud backup. They are carried over by a direct phone-to-phone transfer on Android 12 and later. On older versions of Android they are not carried at all. If they matter to you on Android, choose “Iris + Gallery” when you take them.

How do I get rid of one?

Open the photograph full-screen and delete it there. Retaking a photograph replaces the old one.

4Iris, the coach

What is she?

An artificial-intelligence coach. Her replies are generated by large language models, not written by a person, and nobody reads them before you do. She can help you design a habit, set a goal, work out why a week went badly, and answer questions about the application.

What does she know about me?

Your display name, the conversation you are having, and a calculated summary of your tracking: your habit names, when they are scheduled, what you have done and missed this week, your consistency over recent weeks, your streaks and your fuel level. Your goals in the words you used. One habit description, when a message names exactly one habit. That is all — she does not see your email address, your photographs or anybody else’s data.

Is she a therapist?

No, and she must not be used as one. Iris does not diagnose, treat or advise on any health condition. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line — and if you write something that suggests you are, Iris will say so and point you to a person rather than trying to handle it herself. That message is not sent to any AI company and is not kept.

She said something wrong about the app

It happens, and we want to hear about it. Send us what you asked and what she said. She is told a great deal about how Iris works, but she is not reading the source code.

Can I delete a conversation?

Starting a new conversation closes the old one. Conversations are held on our server and the application does not read them back to you — which also means there is no screen where you can delete one individually. Deleting your account deletes all of them. If you want a conversation gone before that, write to us and we will remove it.

5Talking to her

How do I use voice?

Open Iris and tap the microphone. She listens, then she speaks — one at a time, not both at once, which is deliberate: it keeps her from talking over you.

Does my voice leave the phone?

On iPhone, no. Your speech is turned into text on the device itself, always, and only the text is sent. If your language cannot be transcribed on the device, voice reports itself unavailable rather than sending your audio away.

On Android it depends on the phone. On Android 13 and later with a language model installed, transcription happens on the device. Otherwise your phone’s own speech service does it, which means the audio goes to that service — usually Google’s. Iris tells you which is happening when you open voice mode.

Either way, the recording is never saved to a file and never uploaded by Iris. A single stretch of speech is limited to a minute.

She will not hear me

Check that Iris has permission to use the microphone in your phone’s settings, and on Android that a speech service is installed and has a model for your language. A pair of headphones with their own microphone occasionally confuses the phone; try without them.

Can I change her voice?

Not at present. She has one voice per language, chosen deliberately.

6Friends and competitions

How do I add someone?

Search for their username — you need the username, not their display name. They receive a request and a notification, and nothing is shared until they accept.

What can a friend see?

Your display name, biography and picture, subject to the three visibility settings in your profile. A friend cannot see your habits, your history or your photographs. Only a competition changes that.

What does a competition show the other players?

This is the one place your habits become visible to other people, so it is worth being clear. Every accepted player in a competition can see the names of the habits you entered and, for each day of the round, whether you did them or missed them, along with your points and how many rounds you have won. If you choose to share a photograph in a competition, they can see that too.

Habits you did not enter stay private. So does everything else.

How do competitions work?

You invite friends — they have to be accepted friends first. Everyone who joins locks in the habits they are competing with. A round runs for a week, and at the end of it the highest score takes the round. Shared photographs from a round are deleted once it is decided, and in any case within eight days. A competition holds up to twenty-five players.

Can I leave one?

Yes. Leaving removes your score from it and takes away your access to the other players’ photographs immediately. The competition carries on without you.

Someone is bothering me

Block them, from their profile. Blocking removes you from each other’s friend lists and stops any request or invitation reaching you. If it is worse than a nuisance, write to us.

7Notifications and widgets

What will Iris send me?

Reminders for habits you have scheduled a time for; a friend request; a competition invitation; and news that a competition has moved on. Habit reminders are set by your own phone, not sent from our server, so they arrive whether or not you have a signal.

I am not getting them

Check that notifications are permitted for Iris in your phone’s settings — that is the usual cause. On Android, also check that your phone is not putting Iris to sleep to save battery: look for Battery, then unrestricted or unoptimised, for Iris. If reminders are late rather than absent, that is nearly always battery optimisation.

The widget is not updating

Open the application once; the widget is refreshed from it. If it stays stale, remove the widget and add it again. The widget shows the week from the last time the application ran and holds nothing that is not already on your phone.

8Languages

Which languages does Iris speak?

Ten: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. The whole application is translated, and Iris herself replies in the language you have chosen.

How do I change it?

Menu, then Settings, then Language. It follows your account, so it is the same on every device you sign in on. The first time you install Iris it takes its best guess from your phone.

10Your data

Who receives what?

The privacy policy names all six companies, exactly what each one gets, and why. In short: Google runs the database, the storage and our server; OpenAI and Anthropic generate what Iris types; Google generates what she says; Apple and Google handle sign-in, notifications and payment; and none of them is permitted to train a model on what you send her.

Can I have a copy of my data?

Yes. Write to us and we will send it. We answer within ten working days and we charge nothing.

Nothing is tracked?

Iris does not sell personal data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. We record limited onboarding diagnostics under your account: steps such as starting, choosing a habit or goal, viewing an offer, continuing free, purchasing or saving an account, with timestamps and whether the account is a guest. These records contain no chat text and are removed with the account. RevenueCat subscription information and server security logs are separate. There is no third-party advertising or crash-reporting SDK. We keep at most 200 of these onboarding event records per account.

11When something is broken

Habits are not appearing on my other phone

Both devices need to be signed in to the same account — see the two-accounts answer at the top. Then open Iris on both while they have a connection. Photographs are the exception and never sync: they live on the phone that took them.

Iris will not reply

She needs a connection. If you have one and she is still silent, wait a minute and try again — the models she uses occasionally refuse a request, and she falls back to another one. If it lasts longer than that, tell us what you asked.

Something is in the wrong place after an update

Tell us, with a screenshot and the name of your phone. Iris runs on a great many screen sizes and we cannot see them all.
